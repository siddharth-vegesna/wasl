In Arabic, wasl means both "connection" and "receipt".

The problem
Silence makes people walk.

You send an SOS with no signal. Nothing answers. So you leave your vehicle to look for coverage, and the walk is what kills. Getting a message out is half the problem. Knowing it arrived is the other half.

How it gets deployed
One truck. Six camps. Four weeks.
1 · Pilot
One water contractor, one route, one clinic. The driver does nothing new: the box powers on with the engine.
2 · Route
Every truck on the route, same software. More carriers mean shorter waits, and the forecast learns each truck separately.
3 · Then
Any vehicle as a carrier, a native app for phone-to-phone relay, and a satellite messenger on the truck for life-threatening bundles only.
